# Techno World BD - Official E-Commerce & Wholesale Platform

Techno World BD (https://technoworldbangladesh.com) is a premier omni-channel e-commerce, wholesale, and Dubai/Hong Kong/Russia pre-order import platform designed for high-performance retail and B2B sales in Bangladesh.

## Core Features
1. **Hybrid E-Commerce Engine**: Retail ('Order Now') & Wholesale/Pre-order with minimum order limits, live tier pricing, and 100% advance / COD terms.
2. **Comprehensive Admin ERP**: Full product management with duplicate SKU prevention, auto category tagging, banner reordering, bulk CSV/Excel upload/export, status progression, and multi-tier role authorization.
3. **Advanced Customer Portal**: Interactive orders timeline, cancellation handling, voucher generation with barcode and watermarked PDF/print view, and live SMS OTP authentication.
4. **Automated Bangladesh Logistics**: Dynamic weight-tier courier cost calculation (Inside Dhaka 90 + 20/kg, Outside Dhaka 130 + 20/kg, Office Pickup 0 Tk) with courier sync adapters (Pathao, Steadfast, RedX, Paperfly, Sundarban).
5. **Real-time Courier Fraud Engine**: Risk scoring and delivery success metrics (Safe, Low Risk, High Risk) with customer historical integrity analysis.
6. **Multi-Channel Social & Marketplace Hub**: Integrated Daraz, Bikroy, Othoba, Cartup, ClickBD, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, and WhatsApp channels.
